Roadmap to the Platinum — Vice Got a Price!

Built only from the official trophy descriptions and the counts they name. Do the stages in order and the platinum is a checklist, not a gamble. Watch-out flags are not confirmed missables — the game is not out; they are “do it when you see it” cautions read from the descriptions’ own wording.

Stage 1 — Story: chapters & missions

Play the story in order: Gone Fishing (opening) → Chapter 1 → Chapter 2 → Chapter 3 → Chapter 4 → the final mission (gold). Named mission trophies along the way: Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown and Operation Savage Glory. That is 12 story trophies plus the choices counter.

Stage 2 — Watch-outs before the final mission

Before finishing the story, clear the 9 flagged watch-outs: the Ocean Beach cocktail “in the beginning”, choices made for better or worse, second-time’s-the-charm, the partnership moment, the strange encounter, Bret’s unique-animal hunt, the partner Wanted escape, the VCIA unscheduled departure and the showroom smash-out. Keep a manual save before the finale — general trophy-hunting practice.

Stage 3 — Collectibles & counts

The long numbers: 250 Stunts, gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, 3 Collectible Card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, meditate at 18 spots, discover 100 vehicles, register 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app, and explore all 52 areas of Leonida. Sweep region by region.

Stage 4 — Activities & races

Try all 9 activity types once, win a Top Fun Drone Race at all 14 locations, win 20 different races, beat Cal at fishing, finish an arcade game, complete 50 Challenges, bowl at Kolt & Associates’, and finish all 8 stranger strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone’s final missions included.

Stage 5 — World feats & money

Free-roam feats: rob 5 Bank Vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store unseen, 3 shops back-to-back with your partner, and an illegal operation of each of the 9 gangs; 5 takedowns in one Combat Focus and the Rhino tank. Money: launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 across all shops, and purchase all 8 garage properties.

Stage 6 — 100% → Platinum

Attain 100% Completion (A Long Road, gold) once every group above is done — it is the umbrella over the whole list. When the other 54 are ticked, Vice Got a Price! (platinum) unlocks and you are a Leonida Legend.
