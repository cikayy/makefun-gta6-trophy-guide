Platinum roadmap

Six stages, in the order that wastes the least effort: one honest story run, one big cleanup, and a hard-mode victory lap at the end.

Stage 1 — Play the story once, eyes open

Your first run is for the story trophies: both leads' mission chains, the heist setups and the finale. Two things deserve care while you play. When a branching mission offers an alternate path, take it once for Second Chances. And decide early how you feel about True Ending: it asks you to make every major choice the same way in one playthrough, so pick a philosophy at the first big decision and stick to it. Keep manual saves at chapter breaks — several later stages will thank you for them.

Stage 2 — Clean up the map

With the story done and the whole map open, sweep up everything tied to places: the buried caches in the Keys, spray walls, postcards from every named area, animal photographs, hidden locations and the sunken wreck. Work district by district with the checklist filtered to Collectibles and World, and use your daily-driver car so the miles for Full Tank keep accumulating while you roam.

Stage 3 — Races and activities

Now the skill events: the Leonida Street Series and every street race, the Vice Beach drone races, the wave runner races, sunrise meditations, and gold on every Shooting Range challenge — community trackers list 42 challenges in the range's set, so settle in for a focused session or two. Bullseye's consecutive headshots are easiest to pick up here while your aim is warm.

Stage 4 — Money and property

Turn your attention to the bank balance. Win the big poker hand, buy your first Keys property, and fill a garage collection with distinct vehicles. The ordering matters: hold cash until First Million pops before you start serious spending, then buy properties toward Property Mogul, and clear out one small store's clothing stock for Fashion Forward. Keep a separate save before the spending spree begins.

Stage 5 — The 100% push

Everything left feeds one number. Finish the stranger side missions for Helping Hand, mop up random world events, discover the last hidden locations for No Stone Unturned, and follow the in-game completion screen for whatever remains. When the counter reaches 100%, Completionist is yours — and most of the list should already be ticked off.

Stage 6 — The hard-mode victory lap

The final stage is a fresh playthrough with the difficulty set high and left there: Completionist Prime asks for 100% without ever lowering it, Master of Leonida asks for 100% on the hardest difficulty, and Completionist Eternal simply asks for a second 100% run — so one well-planned victory lap can earn all three together. Never touch the difficulty setting mid-run, and keep a separate save as insurance.
