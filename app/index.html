Platinum roadmap

Six stages, in the order we would play them: one clean story pass, side stories next, then activities, collections, money — and the 100% finish that unlocks the platinum.

Stage 1 — Story first, one clean pass

Play the main story from Just Like Paradise and Paradise Lost through the chapter trophies to You Gotta Know When to Get Off, picking up the named mission trophies along the way — Trust (Breakfast Run), Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist), Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked and Snow Storm. Keep regular manual saves, especially around Actions Speak Louder: its description is only “Choices made (for better or worse)”, so a save before each big decision protects every branch.

Stage 2 — Strangers and side stories

Clear the people stories next: the final-mission trophies for Andy (Road to Rehabilitation), Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand) and Tyrone (Spin On This!). Work the Strangers & Freaks strands as they appear — all 8 strands count toward that silver — and take Bret's request for Fair Game carefully: a unique animal sounds like a one-off encounter, so save before the hunt.

Stage 3 — Activities and medals

Settle into the activity grind: gold medals in The Shootist's Shooting Range challenges (42 are tracked), wins in Top Fun drone races at every location and 20 different race wins for Earn Cash, Not Kudos. Round it out with One Last Thing's 50 Challenges, Baited My Tackle against Cal, Stick Fiddler in the arcade and a follow-through at the bowling alley for Let's Go Bowling, sampling every activity type once for Master of None as you go.

Stage 4 — Collect and explore

Turn the map into a checklist: 250 Cunning Stunts, 3 Collectible Card sets for Don't Touch My Deck, WAiNK It at 100 locations, Discover 100 vehicles for Whip It Good, every meditation spot for Positive Mental Attitude and On the Spot's 10 Scooter Bros registrations. Finish with The Goodtime State — explore all of Leonida until no fog is left.

Stage 5 — Money, heat and heists

Now the risky money: launder Marked Bills up to $250,000 for Dime on the Dollar, spend $100,000 across shops for Treat Yo Self, buy every garage for Little Boxes, rob 5 Bank Vaults for Maximum Withdrawal and chain Retail Therapy's 3 shop robberies with your partner. Several feats here are watch-outs — Hot Together's partner escape among them — so keep a separate save before each attempt.

Stage 6 — The 100% finish

Bring it home with A Long Road: 100% completion, using the in-game tracker to hunt whatever percentage remains. The moment the final trophy lands, Vice Got a Price! — the platinum — is awarded on top. Congratulations, Legend.
